# Surface brief: home (single-page portfolio)

Scope: `/`, the whole site. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads; interface recedes).
Audience and job: recruiters, ML practitioners, and prospective clients deciding in a few minutes who
Jithesh is, what he builds, with what, and how to reach him. Action: open a project repository or
make contact. Proof: two roles, three projects, two resume-supported metrics. Constraint: nothing
beyond the resume; the NIFTY project is technical only.

Decision record: the owner asked for autonomous execution, so no decision page was served. The brief
pins dark, cinematic, technical, and a 3D "AI neural data system" centerpiece; those pins were kept
and the rolled direction (grounded candidate 7, computer-vision annotation overlay) supplies
topology, controls, and state vocabulary. No image generation on this machine: code-led.

Challenger verdicts (all declined on audience identification against the pinned brief):
- Seven-segment display: kept its discipline that the unlit state is designed too. Raise: every
  annotation frame has a resting ghost state, faint corner ticks, never an absent one.
- Glazier colorfield partition: kept "color reserved for what matters now". Raise: the lime is never
  decoration; it appears only on the thing currently locked.
- Multiplane cel dawn: kept travel as camera movement through depth. Raise: scroll moves the camera
  through the system instead of sliding panels over it.
- CRT pixel arcade, Shibuya sleeve, wax-pack cards: declined, nothing transferable without costume.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a model's view of one career: a field of data in which the things that matter
are detected, framed, and labelled. It refuses the glowing particle blob behind a centered name with
glass cards beneath.

OWN-WORLD: Blue-black ink ground, off-white type, one system blue carried by the 3D field, one lime
reserved for whatever is locked. Square corners everywhere. The component language is the annotation
frame: four corner ticks at rest that draw out into a full box on lock, with a label chip on figures
and targets. Wide-set Archivo display, Archivo text, mono only for data.

STORY: The visitor sees raw points become a network become ordered output, reads who built it, then
traces each project as the pipeline it is, and ends at four ways to make contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed 3D system weighted right: scattered points enter from the left, pass
through a five-layer network, and leave as a lattice. Five output nodes carry tracked labels. Name in
two lines at up to 6rem, lower left, role and one sentence beneath, primary action "View Projects"
and secondary "Let's Connect" under that, social links last. Nav pinned top.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Lock-on. The pointer is attention: the nearest labelled output node locks,
its frame closes, turns lime, and the path through the network that feeds it lights. The same lock
grammar drives nav, skill nodes, pipeline stages, and buttons. Motion grammar: exponential ease-out,
ticks draw to a box, signals travel left to right, nothing bounces or spins.

FORM: Computer-vision annotation overlay, position 7 of 7 on the grounded list. Seed key 8502ec46.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
